Privacy Policy – Gerimo Notes

Effective Date: February 19, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Gerimo Notes mobile application (package name: com.antozstudios.gerimonotes, hereby referred to as "Application") created by AntozStudios (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is provided "AS IS".

General Data Collection and Local Processing

The Application is designed as a local-first note-taking and digital whiteboard tool. It does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) on servers owned or operated by the Service Provider.

The Application does not require user registration or account creation and maintains no central database of user profiles. All content created or managed within the Application—including your notes, drawings, canvas layers, pages, color palettes, custom presets, and settings—is stored exclusively on your local device. This data is not transmitted to the Service Provider and remains under your full control unless you explicitly export or share it using third-party applications or services (e.g., PDF, PNG, SVG, or .GERIMO file exports).

Camera, Photos, and Document Scanning

The Application provides optional features allowing you to capture photos or scan physical documents to import into your notes and whiteboard projects:

  • Document Scanning & QR Scanning: Powered by Google Play Services (ML Kit Document Scanner and Google Code Scanner).
  • Camera & Media Access: Used solely when you choose to take a photo or select image files from your device to attach to a note or page.

All scanned documents, captured photos, and imported media files are processed locally on your device or via standard system APIs. They are stored within the app's local project storage and are never uploaded to or accessible by the Service Provider.

In-App Purchases and Billing

The Application offers optional digital purchases (such as "Notes Pro" or "Remove Ads").

  • Purchases are processed entirely through the Google Play Billing API managed by Google LLC.
  • The Service Provider does not collect, receive, or store your credit card details, bank account information, or billing address.
  • Purchase tokens and subscription statuses are verified securely through Google Play Services. For details on how Google handles your payment data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Device Information, Analytics, and System Updates

The Application itself does not embed independent third-party analytics or telemetry trackers. However, standard technical and aggregate information (such as installation counts, device model, operating system version, crash logs, and language/region) may be automatically gathered and made available to the Service Provider by app store platforms (e.g., Google Play Console) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Additionally, the Application uses standard Google Play libraries (such as Google Play In-App Updates and In-App Reviews) to facilitate seamless app updates and optional user ratings through the Google Play Store interface.

Advertising and Data Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

This Application uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob is an advertising platform operated by Google LLC. To present relevant ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device and usage identifiers.

Data collected by AdMob may include:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, screen parameters, language settings)
  • Approximate location data (network-derived) for location-based ad relevance
  • Interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, and viewability metrics)

GDPR & EU User Consent (Google UMP)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK), the Application uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request your explicit consent before displaying personalized advertisements, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google's EU User Consent Policy.

  • Managing / Changing Consent: You can modify or revoke your consent preferences at any time within the Application by navigating to Settings > Privacy > Change Consent.
  • Device-level Opt-Out: You can also opt out of personalized advertising globally on your Android device under Settings > Google > Ads by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or choosing "Delete advertising ID".

For more information regarding Google's ad data practices, please visit:

  • Google Partner Sites Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

The Application integrates specific third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC:

  • Google AdMob & Google UMP (Ad serving & privacy consent management)
  • Google Play Services (In-App Purchases, In-App Review, In-App Update, ML Kit Document Scanner, Code Scanner)

Any interactions with these underlying services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The Service Provider does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

Communication

If you choose to contact the Service Provider directly (e.g., via email or through official community channels like Discord), any personal information provided (such as your email address or message content) will be used solely to answer your questions, handle bug reports, or provide support. Such correspondence is handled outside the Application.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the Application does not transmit or store your personal data on external servers controlled by the Service Provider, there is no central user data to retain or delete.

All local project data, notes, and app settings remain stored on your device until you manually delete individual projects, clear the app data via Android device settings, or uninstall the Application.

Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in jurisdictions requiring higher age limits for data consent without parental approval). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, advertising content ratings are set to "Teen" (T) or appropriate family levels, with child-directed flags configured accordingly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has inadvertently provided personal information to the Service Provider via email or other direct channels, please contact us at antozstudios@proton.me so we can delete the correspondence promptly.

Security

The Service Provider is committed to protecting your privacy. Since all user-generated content remains locally on your device, its security is primarily maintained by Android's built-in sandbox security model. For any support communication received via email, reasonable organizational measures are taken to prevent unauthorized access.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect app updates or legal compliance requirements. Any revisions will be published with a revised "Effective Date". You are advised to review this Privacy Policy regularly.
